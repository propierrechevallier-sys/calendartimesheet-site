Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026, updated 14 October 2026 (new section: how your data is protected). Applies to the Calendar Timesheet add-on for Google Sheets, the ready-made Calendar Timesheet sheet, and this website.

In short. Calendar Timesheet runs inside your own Google account. It reads your calendars, writes to the Google Sheet you use it in, and sends nothing to us. The only data that ever leaves your Google account is your licence key, sent to Gumroad to check it. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no server of ours.

Who is responsible

Calendar Timesheet is published by Pierre Chevallier, entrepreneur individuel, France ("we"). Contact: support@calendartimesheet.com.

What the add-on reads from your Google account

Google dataWhat is readWhy
Your calendar listName, colour, time zone and identifier of each calendar you can seeTo let you choose which calendars hold billable time
Events of the calendars you choose, only for the periods being calculatedTitle, start and end, colour, "busy or free", event type, status, and the guests and organiser (email address, your own response, whether a guest is a meeting room)To count hours, match each event to a client by colour, calendar, title word or guest email domain, skip declined invitations, and count an event present in two calendars once

The add-on never reads event descriptions, locations, attachments or video-call links, never reads your email, contacts or files, and never creates, edits or deletes a calendar event.

Where your data is kept

  • In your spreadsheet: the tabs the add-on writes (This month, Dashboard, monthly statements per client, Payments, To sort, Export, Log). Event titles appear there; guest email addresses appear only in the To sort tab, for events no rule recognised.
  • In Google's storage for the add-on (Apps Script properties, inside your Google account): your settings, clients, rates and rules for that spreadsheet, and, for your account, the dates of your trial and your licence key with its last check.

Everything stays in your Google account. We have no access to it and keep no copy. It is deleted when you delete the spreadsheet; removing the add-on's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions stops all reading and writing.

What leaves your Google account

  • Your licence key, if you enter one, with the product identifier, sent to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com) when you activate it and then about once a week, to check that the purchase is valid. Gumroad answers whether it is valid, refunded or ended. Nothing about your calendar is sent.
  • Emails to you: the monthly summary, retainer alerts and the trial reminder are sent from your own Google account to your own address, through Google. You can turn them off in the add-on's settings.

Nothing else is sent anywhere. The add-on contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code.

How your data is protected

  • No server, no copy. The add-on's code runs on Google's Apps Script platform, under your own Google account. Your calendar data goes from Google Calendar to your spreadsheet inside Google's infrastructure and never passes through a server of ours, because we have none. We cannot see, copy or export your data.
  • Least privilege. Calendar access is read-only: the add-on cannot create, edit or delete an event. Spreadsheet access is limited to the spreadsheet you use the add-on in (Google's "current spreadsheet only" permission), not your other files. Email access is send-only and goes to your own address: the add-on cannot read your mailbox.
  • Network locked to one address. The add-on's manifest allows outgoing requests to a single host, api.gumroad.com, and Apps Script blocks requests to any other address. Only your licence key and the product identifier are sent there, over HTTPS, so they are encrypted in transit.
  • Encryption by Google. Your spreadsheet and the add-on's stored settings are kept by Google, which encrypts customer content stored at rest (see Google's default encryption at rest and how Google Workspace uses encryption).
  • Access control. Only your Google account runs the add-on on your data. The tabs it writes can be seen only by the people you share the spreadsheet with, like any other content of that spreadsheet. Your licence key and trial dates are kept in storage private to your account (Apps Script "user properties"), which people you share the spreadsheet with cannot read.
  • Data minimisation. Only the fields listed above are read. Event descriptions, locations, attachments and video-call links are never read. Guest email addresses are written to the sheet only in the To sort tab, for events no rule recognised.
  • No human access, no resale. No person reads your data, it is not used to train any model, never sold and never used for advertising.
  • Retention and deletion. Data is kept only as long as you keep it: deleting the spreadsheet deletes its tabs and the add-on's settings for it, and removing the add-on's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions stops all reading and writing at once. Purchase records we receive from Gumroad are deleted on request.
  • Security issues. If we learn of a vulnerability in the add-on, we publish a fix as quickly as possible and tell affected customers at the email address used for their purchase. You can report a security issue to support@calendartimesheet.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Calendar Timesheet's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, is never transferred to anyone, never used for advertising, never sold, and never read by a person.

Purchases

Licences are sold by Gumroad, which acts as the seller of record and processes your payment details, invoice and taxes under its own privacy policy. We receive from Gumroad the information needed to manage your purchase (such as your email address and what you bought), which we use only for that purpose and for support.

This website

This website sets no cookies and contains no analytics or tracking. Its fonts are served from this site. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Support emails

If you write to us, we use your message and address only to answer you, and delete them within 24 months of the last exchange.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you (in practice, support emails and purchase records received from Gumroad), and you may complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL. Data kept in your own Google account is under your control: you can edit or delete it at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its date. A change that widens what the add-on reads would require a new authorisation from you in Google's consent screen.
