    How to export your Google Calendar

    1. On a computer, open Google Calendar. Google's help page notes that the phone app cannot export.
    2. Open Settings, then Import & export, then Export. A .zip with one .ics file per calendar downloads.
    3. Drop that .zip above. There is no need to unzip it. To export a single calendar instead, open its Settings and sharing page and choose Export calendar.

    You can export a calendar if you have the "Make changes and manage sharing" permission on it. The export holds the whole history of each calendar; the calculator keeps only the period you choose.

    What the calculator counts

    • Recurring meetings occurrence by occurrence, with the dates you removed, moved or cancelled taken into account.
    • Only the part inside the period: a meeting that crosses midnight at the end of the period, or the end of a week, is split.
    • A meeting found in two calendars once.
    • All-day events and declined invitations left out, unless you untick them. Time marked free can be left out too.
    • Times in your browser's time zone, shown with the results.

    Questions

    Is my calendar uploaded?

    No. The page has no server behind it: your browser opens the file, adds up the durations and shows the result. Nothing is stored or sent, and closing the tab forgets everything. The privacy policy says the same.

    Why are there no totals by colour?

    Google Calendar's export leaves event colours out, so no tool can read them from the file. Keywords in the title or one calendar per client work instead. Calendar Timesheet reads colours directly from your calendar; the guide how to count hours in Google Calendar per client, colour or week compares the methods.

    Can I see the total on my phone?

    Google Calendar's export only works on a computer. For a total you can read anywhere, see Can Google Calendar calculate hours worked?

    Can I get the list of events in Excel?

    The calculator shows totals only. For one line per event, drop the same export into the free Google Calendar to Excel and CSV converter.

    Is this like Time Insights?

    Time Insights counts meetings with other guests, and only some Google Workspace editions have it. The calculator counts every event, solo work blocks included, from any Google account. See Google Calendar Time Insights: where it is, why it's missing.

    How do I round these hours for an invoice?

    Paste the durations into the billable hours calculator: it rounds each one up to 6 or 15 minutes, applies a minimum and an hourly rate, and converts everything to decimal hours.
