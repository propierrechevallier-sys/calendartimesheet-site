You open your calendar, go back four weeks and count each client's meetings. You copy dates and durations into a spreadsheet, round them and multiply by the rate.

The details that make the hours right

Counting hours from a calendar sounds simple until a meeting crosses midnight or sits in two calendars.

Only hours inside the month

A meeting across midnight or the end of the month is split at the boundary.

Counted once

The same meeting in two of your calendars counts once.

Declined meetings left out

Invitations you declined and all-day events are skipped; time marked free can be too.

Rounding and minimums

Exact time or rounded up to 5, 6, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, with an optional minimum per meeting.

Prepaid hours

Monthly or one-off retainers, with an email at 80% and 100%.

Notes that stay

Lines and notes you add by hand survive every update.

Questions

Where does my data go?

Into your own Google Sheet, in your Google account. The only thing that ever leaves it is your licence key, sent to Gumroad to check it. No analytics, no tracking. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it change my calendar?

No. It only reads it.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your statements stay in the sheet and export keeps working for free. Autopilot, clients and rates need a licence.

Why does Google say it hasn't verified the app?

During early access, Calendar Timesheet comes as a ready-made sheet that you copy. The copy belongs to you, so Google shows its standard warning for scripts it has not reviewed. The step-by-step page shows each screen.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time from your Gumroad receipt. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. See managing your subscription.

Does it work with Google Workspace accounts?

Yes, with personal Google accounts and Google Workspace accounts, as long as your organisation allows add-ons and scripts.
