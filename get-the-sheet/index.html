Install Calendar Timesheet

About three minutes. Free export forever; the Pro trial lasts until your first month-end statement, 14 days at least. No card.

Calendar Timesheet is an add-on for Google Sheets™, available from the Google Workspace Marketplace. Google reviewed its access to your calendar before publishing it. Once installed, it updates itself.

Step by step

  1. Install it. Click the badge above. On the Marketplace page, click Install, choose your Google account, read the permissions and click Allow.
  2. Open a spreadsheet. Open any spreadsheet in Google Sheets, or create a new one at sheets.new.
  3. Start the setup. Open the menu Extensions, then Calendar Timesheet, then Open. The panel opens on the right: tick your calendars, name your clients, turn on autopilot.

Already installed? Start at step 2. If Calendar Timesheet is not in the Extensions menu, reload the spreadsheet; if it is still missing, open Extensions, then Add-ons, then Manage add-ons, and next to Calendar Timesheet click Options, then Use in this document.
