Make a copy of Calendar Timesheet

Early access. Calendar Timesheet comes as a ready-made sheet with the add-on built in. You copy it into your own Google Drive; from then on it belongs to you and runs only in your account.

Step by step

  1. Make a copy. The button above opens Google Sheets and asks "Copy document?". Click Make a copy. The copy lands in your Google Drive.
  2. Open the add-on. In your copy, open the menu Extensions, then Calendar Timesheet, then Open. If the menu is not there yet, wait a few seconds or reload the page.
  3. Authorise it. Google shows "Authorisation required". Click Continue and pick your Google account.
  4. Get past Google's warning. Google shows "Google hasn't verified this app". Click Advanced, then Go to Calendar Timesheet (unsafe). The section below explains why this screen appears.
  5. Review the permissions listed by Google and click Allow. The table below says what each one is for.
  6. Run the setup. The panel opens on the right: tick your calendars, name your clients, turn on autopilot.

Why Google shows a warning

When you copy a sheet that contains a script, the copy and its script belong to you. Google shows the same warning for every script it has not reviewed, including the ones people write for themselves. It does not mean anything was found wrong.

You can read every line of the code before allowing it: in your copy, open Extensions, then Apps Script.
